Multiomics and Precision Medicine Joint Conference (MOPM)

多體學與精準醫學聯合會議（MOPM）於 2019 年由台灣生物資訊暨系統生物學會、台灣蛋白體學會與台灣基因醫學暨生物標記學會共同發起。2020 年起，台灣演化與計算生物學會、台灣精準醫學學會及台灣胞外體學會加入。會議每年舉辦一屆，由六個學會輪流主辦。

MOPM was founded in 2019 by the Taiwan Bioinformatics and Systems Biology Society, the Taiwan Proteomics Society, and the Taiwan Genomic Medicine and Biomarker Society. In 2020, the Taiwan Society of Evolution and Computational Biology, the Taiwan Precision Medicine Society, and the Taiwan Society for Extracellular Vesicles joined. The conference is held once a year, hosted in turn by the six societies.
